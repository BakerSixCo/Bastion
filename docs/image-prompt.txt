TIPS
=====================================================================

- Generators mangle written text: keep "no readable text" in the
  prompt, or expect gibberish labels. Add real labels afterward.
- If people or logos appear anyway, append: "no people, no text,
  no logos".
- "Starlink" as a word sometimes triggers logo/brand rendering; if the
  dish comes out branded or wrong, swap the phrase for "small white
  flat rectangular satellite internet dish on a kickstand".
- Wiring will come out as decorative spaghetti. Treat any generated
  image as decoration — docs/wiring-diagram.svg and
  docs/deployed-illustration.svg remain the accurate references.
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 for a poster/cover, 4:3 or 1:1 for the binder.
- Midjourney users: append "--ar 16:9 --style raw" to any prompt.
